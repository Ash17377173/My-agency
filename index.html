Main Market Categories (Muscat, Oman)
Category 1 Beauty & wellness — dental clinics, cosmetics clinics, hair salons. High visual content demand, reputation-driven, strong word-of-mouth culture, loyal repeat customers. Instagram and Snapchat primary.
Category 2 Food & beverage — restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens. Highest posting frequency, trend-sensitive, driven by aesthetics and offers. Heavily influenced by food influencers and Google Maps reviews.
Category 3 General SME services — retail, education, real estate, fitness. Less social media savvy, often under-served by agencies. Willing to pay for results but skeptical due to bad past experiences.

Market Gaps — Your Opportunity White Space
No sector-specialist social media agency exists for SMEs in Muscat:

Dental, salon, and restaurant clients all have unique content needs. Nobody is serving them with deep category expertise.

Arabic + English bilingual strategy is underserved:

Most agencies prioritize English. Muscat's audience is majority Arabic-speaking. Culturally fluent, bilingual content is a real gap.

Performance accountability is missing at the SME level:

SME clients have been burned by vanity metrics (likes, followers) instead of real business outcomes like appointments or walk-ins.

Unique Value Proposition

"We help dental clinics, salons, and restaurants in Muscat attract more customers through social media — with bilingual content, sector expertise, and real results you can measure."
